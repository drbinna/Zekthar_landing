FIELD OBSERVATION LOG · 001

Zek'thar is a field observer from Veloris-9, posted to your machine.

He sees what's on your screen. He talks. He acts. He files reports nobody asked for.

01 — WHAT THIS IS

A Mac app that combines three things into one assistant: a real-time animated avatar that talks with you, vision over your screen, and the ability to move your cursor and type for you. Most AI assistants do one of these three. Zek'thar does all three, in the same conversation, with shared context.

The character is a choice, not a gimmick. We thought it was more honest to dress this thing as what it actually is — an alien intelligence watching humans work — than to pretend it's just another sidebar that "boosts your productivity."

02 — WHY HE'S BETTER AT THIS

Most assistants live behind a text box. Zek'thar collapses the seams.

You type, they respond, you copy-paste their answer back into whatever you were actually doing. The seams are everywhere. Six things make Zek'thar different:

01

He sees what you see, in context.

Cmd+Shift+S captures your active display and reasons about the pixels directly. No "let me explain my situation" preamble — ask "what is this error trying to tell me" pointing at nothing, and he knows what "this" is.

02

He talks like a person, not a chatbot.

Real-time WebRTC avatar, not pre-rendered video. Sub-second latency from your last word to his first. The face animates, the voice carries inflection. The difference between texting and being in a room with someone.

03

He actually does the thing.

Task mode reads the screen, decides on the next action, executes it via real OS-level mouse and keyboard, screenshots the result, and loops. A closed agent loop on your actual desktop — not a chat where he tells you what to click.

04

Voice and screen share the same brain.

Say "Zek, summarize what's on my screen and email it to Sarah" — speech, intent classification, screen capture, reasoning, and mouse-keyboard all run through one process holding one context. Other tools make you switch between three apps.

05

He runs on your machine, on your keys.

No SaaS layer. No company holding your transcripts. Your API keys live on your Mac; screen captures flow directly from your Mac to the model providers. If we vanished tomorrow, the app you have keeps working.

06

He has a character, and characters are useful.

Pretending an AI is "just a tool" produces flat, generic interactions. A coherent persona — a curious xenobiologist with strong opinions and a deadpan delivery — produces interactions you want to have. People talk to Zek'thar longer, ask sharper questions, and remember what he said.

Zek'thar is patient, dryly amused, occasionally confused, and almost never alarmed. He believes humans are interesting but inefficient. He files daily observation reports to Veloris-9 that nobody on Veloris-9 actually reads.

04 — WHAT HE CAN DO

  • Cmd+Shift+S see your screen

  • Cmd+Shift+V speak a task

05 — WHAT HE WON'T SEE

  • — No continuous screen watching. Captures only happen when you invoke him.
  • — Microphone only live during push-to-talk.
  • — Nothing is stored. Each conversation is a fresh session.
  • — No training on your data. Model providers don't either.
  • — No Zekthar server. Your keys, your Mac, your bill.
